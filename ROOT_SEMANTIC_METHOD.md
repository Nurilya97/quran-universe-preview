# Quran Universe — Root Semantic Reconstruction Method

> **Normative authority:** `.github/skills/quran-universe/SKILL.md` is the canonical project skill. This document is a human-readable research reference. If the two ever diverge, update this document to match the master skill; do not create a second root-research authority.

## Purpose

This is the working research skill for Arabic root analysis in Quran Universe.

The goal is not to collect translations of derivatives. The goal is to reconstruct the **fundamental semantic image / mechanism of the root** that explains why these different words belong to the same root family.

## Golden rule

A shared translation does not imply a shared Arabic meaning.

If several Arabic roots can all be translated as “remain”, “know”, “protect”, “see”, etc., compare them. Ask why this particular root was chosen in this particular usage and what semantic mechanism distinguishes it from neighbouring roots.

Likewise, when one root has many dictionary glosses, do not treat them as unrelated by default. Test whether one fundamental image can explain them consistently.

## Research workflow

1. **Collect the family broadly**
   - Gather classical and modern dictionary entries and derivatives.
   - Include concrete, bodily, material, botanical, architectural, idiomatic and abstract senses.
   - Root-verify every form. Broad search results may mix homographs, neighbouring roots or cross-references.

2. **Start from concrete images**
   - Physical meanings often preserve the mechanism more clearly than abstract translations.
   - Ask what is physically happening, what the object does, where it is located, and why this word would name it.

3. **Find the fundamental meaning**
   - Search for the smallest semantic mechanism that explains the largest number of verified derivatives.
   - Do not stop at a vague noun such as “core”, “protection” or “movement” if the derivatives reveal a dynamic mechanism.
   - Example of the desired level: not merely “core”, but “reach the defining core without stopping at the outer layer, and remain with it”.

4. **Explain every derivative through the root**
   For each form record:
   - dictionary meaning;
   - literal situation or image;
   - connection to the fundamental root mechanism;
   - what is still uncertain.

   “A separate lexical branch” is not a final explanation. It is only a temporary status when the connection has not yet been reconstructed.

5. **Compare apparent synonyms**
   - Compare other Arabic roots that receive the same translation.
   - Identify what kind of “remaining”, “knowing”, “seeing”, “holding”, etc. this root specifically expresses.
   - This comparison is part of testing the root mechanism.

6. **Separate root meaning from morphological form**
   Ask two different questions:
   - What does the root contribute?
   - What does the morphological pattern/form do to that root meaning?

   Do not use generic labels such as “intensive” or “causative” as the whole explanation. Show how the form transforms this root’s concrete mechanism in its attested usages.

7. **Test the Quranic corpus**
   - Examine every Quranic occurrence of the root/form when the set is manageable.
   - Look for repeated operations, contrasts and rhetorical environments.
   - Actively search for a verse that would contradict the proposed root mechanism.

8. **Falsification standard**
   A connection is not accepted because of one attractive coincidence.
   It becomes systemically supported when the same mechanism:
   - explains the verified derivatives;
   - survives comparison with neighbouring roots;
   - fits the morphology;
   - fits the Quranic contexts;
   - does not create internal contradictions.

9. **Evidence levels**
   Keep these distinct:
   - **Attested fact** — directly supported by dictionaries, corpus, grammar or text.
   - **Systemically supported semantic connection** — reconstructed from multiple converging usages with no contradiction found.
   - **Historical etymology** — only claim this when historical/early evidence directly supports the development.

10. **Reader-facing test**
    The explanation should produce the reaction:
    **“Now I understand why this word is called that.”**
    If the reader still has to memorise a disconnected list of meanings, the analysis is not finished.

## Output format for a root

### Root
- Fundamental image / semantic nucleus.
- One short dynamic formula.

### Root galaxy
- Derivatives remain visible as words in the root space, not dumped into the root-info panel.
- Quran-attested forms are visually distinguishable.
- Verb-form numbers describe verbal derivational families; noun patterns remain their own morphology.

### Each derivative
- Dictionary meaning.
- Connection to the root.
- Morphological-form effect where relevant.
- Quran occurrence status.
- Sources.

### Quran validation
- Show the recurring mechanism across the occurrences.
- Note any occurrence that challenges the model.
- Do not turn a plausible image into a historical claim without evidence.

## Current calibrated example: ل ب ب

Working fundamental mechanism:

**ل ب ب — сердцевина / определяющая внутренняя точка → дойти именно до неё, не остановившись на внешнем или промежуточном → удержаться при ней до конца.**

This model is currently supported by the convergence of:
- لُبّ as core/kernel/pure or choicest part;
- the action of reaching the kernel of an almond;
- لبيب as a person who reaches the essence in understanding;
- the chest / لَبَّة line as a precise bodily point;
- staying/keeping to a place or matter without leaving it;
- the breast-girth لَبَب, named through the chest area and functionally holding the saddle in position;
- Quranic ألباب contexts that repeatedly move from outward appearance or information toward essence, discernment, remembrance and consequent choice.

Important caution:
The breast-girth is lexically named from the chest-area term; its holding function is a meaningful semantic resonance, but should not be presented as the historically proven reason for the name unless a source establishes that explicitly.
